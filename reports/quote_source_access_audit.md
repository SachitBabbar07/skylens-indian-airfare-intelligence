## Operational decision

There is no approved source in `config/quote_permissions.example.json`. The implemented offline importer makes no network request. It accepts a user-supplied, source-authorized CSV only after a reviewer records collection/storage rights, domains, acquisition method, valid period, retention deadline, source-backed daily limit and no-cost confirmation, bound to a permission document hash. ML permission is an additional gate. Terms must be rechecked before use; this audit does not grant rights or sign a contract. Do not open paid production accounts or contact providers automatically.

Manual workflow availability means the **software** is ready; it does not authorize manual copying from the carriers above. The next acquisition dependency is an actual source permission grant or an independently licensed authentic quote dataset with complete semantics.
