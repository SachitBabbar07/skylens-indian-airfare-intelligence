# Verified one-way data feasibility

Required target: actual Indian domestic one-way ticket for one adult, all mandatory taxes/fees included, INR, documented cabin, travel date and observation time. **Qualified observations: 0.**

The new APIx/HybridAni files provide contemporary-looking route/time fields but fail the documented target/reuse requirements. Kaggle candidates are derivatives without the missing definitions. Foreign academic data, tariffs, indices, sandbox examples and synthetic fares are not replacements.

SerpApi lists [250 free monthly searches](https://serpapi.com/pricing) and [passenger/journey controls](https://serpapi.com/google-flights-api), but no account/key, authenticated response, complete fee evidence or applicable retained-training-data rights was available. Its [legal terms](https://serpapi.com/legal) place downstream lawful use on the user; provider marketing is not a data license. No signup, paid upgrade or quote request occurred.

Manual quote CSV/schema downloads are implemented in the app. Offline validation/ingestion preserves source permission, evidence hashes, unknown fields and original values; records never enter training automatically. The exact blocker is source-backed target semantics and permitted access/reuse, followed by sufficient independent data for evaluation.
